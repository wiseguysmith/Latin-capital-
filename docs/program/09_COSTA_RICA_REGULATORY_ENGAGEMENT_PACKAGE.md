## 1. Framing — consultation, not exemption

The official **Centro de Innovación Financiera (CIF)** is a joint consultation and dialogue channel of the financial supervisors and BCCR, designed to help innovators understand how regulation applies. It is **not** a license or a general regulatory waiver (`03_...` §7.1). The launch plan must **not** assume a "sandbox exemption." The CIF is used to present the operating diagram, ask which regulator owns each component, obtain documented feedback, request a SUGEVAL classification discussion, confirm whether SUGEF views any activity as intermediation, confirm whether AML-only registration applies, and document that capitalYA proactively approached regulators.

## 2. Which authorities matter, and for what

| Authority | Relevance to the MVP | Launch-blocking? |
|---|---|---|
| **CIF** | Initial **coordinated** point of engagement; routes questions to the right supervisor | No — it's the entry door |
| **SUGEF** | Most relevant: platform-vs-lender distinction, financial-intermediation questions, lender responsibilities, AML registration, credit-information (CIC/CICOC) access, servicing structure | Potentially — the intermediation question must be answered |
| **SUGEVAL** | Relevant when capitalYA introduces tokens, investor solicitation, receivable participations, or private/public offerings | **No** for a closed MVP with **no investors and no transferable instruments** |
| **BCCR** | Relevant if capitalYA wants direct payment-system access, operates payment accounts, controls settlement, or integrates directly with SINPE | **Less central** — the **lender owns payments/settlement** in the MVP |

For the recommended MVP, the **lender owns payments and settlement**, which keeps BCCR peripheral to initial approval, and there are **no investors or tokens**, which keeps SUGEVAL non-blocking. The live question is SUGEF's view of the platform-vs-lender line.

## 4. Questions to submit (specific — never "is capitalYA legal?")

A broad question invites a broad non-answer. Ask precise ones (aligned to `03_...` §17):

1. Does the described platform activity constitute **financial intermediation**?
2. Does capitalYA require authorization when it **never receives or controls loan funds**?
3. May capitalYA charge the identified **technology and success fees**?
4. Which entity must perform **final KYC**?
5. Does capitalYA require **AML-only registration**?
6. May CRP provide the described **private readiness assessment** without registering as a rating agency?
7. Does the **lender remain the sole credit provider** under the proposed workflow?
8. Does the proposed **servicing model** create a regulated activity?
9. Does the proposed **future token** represent a security?
10. Would the proposed **future institutional offering** qualify as private?
11. Which authority would regulate **transfer or secondary trading**?
12. What additional permissions are required before the **tokenized phase**?

For a **formal complex consultation to SUGEVAL**, the published requirements include a signed filing, identification of the submitting party, a detailed description of the question, and — where applicable — the technical criterion supporting the request.

## 5. Submission sequence (5 stages)

### Stage 1 — Counsel validation
Counsel reviews: platform perimeter, fee structure, partner allocation of duties, CRP classification, data processing, consumer disclosures.

### Stage 2 — CIF engagement
Submit the complete concept package (§3) and request a coordinated meeting.

### Stage 3 — Targeted regulator questions
Based on CIF direction: **SUGEF** receives the lending-platform questions; **SUGEVAL** receives the future investment/token questions; **BCCR** receives payment questions only where necessary.

### Stage 4 — MVP launch decision
Proceed **only when**: counsel confirms the narrowed model; the lender signs the responsibility matrix; no unresolved regulator concern blocks the activity; **funds never touch capitalYA**; **tokenization remains disabled**.

### Stage 5 — Future token classification
If the eventual private offering by one issuer or economic group **exceeds US$1 million**, Costa Rican private-offer **accreditation** requirements must be evaluated. SUGEVAL accreditation is **not** approval, supervision, or a solvency endorsement, and privately offered securities **cannot trade** in the registered secondary market (`03_...` §6.1).

## 7. Dependencies & do-not-cross reminders

- The package **cannot** claim sandbox protection or a waiver (`03_...` §16.13).
- Tokenization is presented as **future phase**, never as part of the MVP ask.
- Every diagram must show **funds never touching capitalYA** and the **lender as sole creditor** (`05`).
- Nothing here is settled until counsel confirms it in writing (`03_...` §17).
