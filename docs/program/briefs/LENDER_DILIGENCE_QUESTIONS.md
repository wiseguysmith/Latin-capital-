# Prospective-Lender Diligence Questions

*For the founder's meetings with shortlisted supervised Costa Rican lenders. Companion to `LENDER_PITCH_ONE_PAGER.md` and the two mockups. 2026-07-19.*

---

Ask in order. Question 1 is pass/fail — if the answer is no, the rest doesn't matter.

## 1. Existential (deal-killers)

1. **Authorization** — Are you currently authorized and supervised to originate these products (invoice factoring, SME working-capital loans) at $5K–$50K tickets? SUGEF status? Any enforcement history or standing issues?
2. **Sole creditor** — Will you be the sole creditor of record — underwrite, decide, set terms, sign the contract, disburse, collect, report — and sign a responsibility matrix (`../10` §4) that says so in writing?

## 2. Credit fit

3. **Appetite** — Sectors, ticket range, risk tolerance, target pricing, product mix (factoring vs. term)?
4. **Current cost** — What does underwriting an SME file cost you today in time and staff? (Where CRP's value lands.)
5. **Readiness input** — Are you comfortable receiving a third-party readiness assessment as *decision-support input* to your own independent underwriting — not a rating, not an approval?

## 3. Operations

6. **Portal vs. API** — Can your staff work applications through a capitalYA portal (record KYC, decisions, terms, disbursement), or do you need an API from day one? (Portal-first is our MVP — `../06`.)
7. **People** — Who would be the underwriter of record and the MLRO on the pilot?
8. **SLAs** — What review turnaround can you commit to (proposed: initial review 2 business days; decision 3–5 after a complete file — negotiated, not imposed)?
9. **Funds & reconciliation** — Can you disburse from and collect into your own or a regulated processor's accounts, and send a daily reconciliation file? (capitalYA never touches funds — `../05`.)

## 4. Compliance & consumer

10. **AML of record** — You own KYC of record and suspicious-transaction filing. Capacity to do it? Comfortable with capitalYA doing complementary, non-relied pre-screening and escalating to your MLRO (`../14`)?
11. **Consumer practices** — Your disclosure, interest-cap compliance, and complaint-handling practices (`../11`)?
12. **Data** — Comfortable with the Ley 8968 consent + US-cloud processing model (`../13`), with your KYC data controlled by you?

## 5. Commercial

13. **Economics** — Fee expectations, and how you view the split between your interest/origination revenue and capitalYA's fixed service fees (`../18`)?
14. **Pilot terms** — Would you accept a closed pilot: limited loan count, ~90 days, no exclusivity required?
15. **Volume** — Realistic origination appetite if the pilot succeeds?

## 6. Resilience

16. **Continuity** — Would you serve as, or help identify, a backup servicer? Comfortable with wind-down provisions (`../16`) that keep borrowers protected if the partnership ends?
17. **Financial standing** — Balance-sheet capacity to fund the pilot book; any concentration or liquidity constraints we should know about?

## Framing to use

- Lead with **their** cost problem (Q4), not our technology.
- Show the two mockups — borrower intake and the lender portal — so "near-zero integration" is concrete.
- Close on **scarcity**: one partner for the Costa Rica pilot. It sorts the serious from the curious.

## Your own red-flags checklist (score after each meeting)

- [ ] Actually licensed for these exact products (not "close enough")
- [ ] Accepts sole-creditor responsibility without trying to push credit risk or decisions onto capitalYA
- [ ] Has staff who can operate a portal within weeks
- [ ] Can move and reconcile funds on their side
- [ ] Real AML/KYC capacity of record
- [ ] Fee expectations compatible with fixed-service-fee model (not demanding a share that recreates creditor economics on our side)
- [ ] No regulatory or financial-standing red flags
- [ ] Willing to engage regulators (CIF/SUGEF) jointly, proactively
