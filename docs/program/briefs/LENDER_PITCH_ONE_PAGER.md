# capitalYA — Partner Lender One-Pager

*For: supervised Costa Rican lenders (banks, financieras) evaluating a pilot partnership. 2026-07-19.*

---

## The pitch in three sentences

**capitalYA sends you pre-organized, pre-verified, assessment-backed SME borrowers — and you keep the entire lending relationship.** Our Capital Readiness Platform (CRP) does the expensive part of your funnel: document collection, verification, readiness scoring, and remediation, so files arrive complete and cheap to underwrite. You make every credit decision, set every term, hold every colón — we are technology, not a competitor.

## The problem we solve *for you*

| Your cost today | With capitalYA |
|---|---|
| 60–90 days to underwrite an SME file | Complete, evidence-backed file at handoff — target initial review in 2 business days |
| Incomplete applications churn your analysts | Files arrive validated against **your** intake checklist |
| Sub-$50K tickets don't cover acquisition + processing cost | Standardized intake + readiness assessment makes small tickets economical |
| Unverifiable claims | Every claim tied to evidence tier, freshness, and a signed assessment with an audit trail |

## What you get

- **A readiness-assessed pipeline** of SME borrowers (invoice factoring / working capital, closed pilot).
- **The Readiness Assessment Package**: 0–100 preparedness score across 8 dimensions, evidence confidence, integrity flags, remediation history, human-approved, signed and versioned.
- **A portal for your team** — review files, request more information, record your decisions and terms. **No engineering integration required to start**; a full API exists when you want it.
- **Servicing technology** and daily reconciliation support — your ledger stays the system of record.

## What stays 100% yours

Final underwriting · approve/decline · rates and terms · the borrower contract · disbursement and collections through **your** accounts · KYC of record and AML reporting · the customer relationship and the interest income.

Our assessment is decision-support only: *not* a credit score, *not* an approval, *not* a guarantee — your independent underwriting governs, contractually and operationally.

## What we never do

We never touch loan funds. We never approve credit. We never guarantee repayment. We take no first-loss and no share of your interest. Our fees are fixed, disclosed technology and service fees — your economics stay your economics.
