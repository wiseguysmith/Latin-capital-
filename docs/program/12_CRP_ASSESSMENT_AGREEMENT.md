## 1. Nature of the service (assessment only)

CRP provides an **informational capital-readiness assessment**: document-completeness analysis, governance and financial-readiness evaluation, data-quality flags, risk-factor identification, a readiness score, remediation recommendations, and an evidence-backed report. The assessment is **decision-support**, used internally by the borrower and shared (on consent) with a lender.

The agreement states expressly (`03_...` §5.1):

> The readiness score is an informational assessment and is not a promise of financing, investment recommendation, credit approval, public credit rating, guarantee of repayment, or substitute for a lender's independent underwriting.

CRP is **not** a rating agency: it does not publish ratings for investment solicitation, does not rate public/tokenized debt, and is not compensated on the rating outcome (`03_...` §5.1).

## 2. Mandatory disclosures to the client (`03_...` §5.2)

- Precise **scope** of the assessment.
- **Data sources** used.
- **Score methodology** at an understandable level (and the methodology version).
- **Assumptions and limitations.**
- **Date** of assessment and **expiration/refresh** date.
- **Human-review** process.
- **Conflict-of-interest** policy.
- **Dispute and correction** process (CRP `57`).
- That the **lender remains responsible for underwriting**.

## 3. Fee

- **Flat fee** `[$2,500–$5,000 TBD]` for document analysis, readiness review, and remediation planning — **billed B2B to the partner lender** under the commercial schedule (`18`), never to the borrower (`21` D-P1). The borrower remains the assessment subject and consent-giver; the lender is the paying client for pipeline assessments.
- **Earned on assessment completion**, not contingent on whether financing closes (`03_...` §5.2).
- **Refund policy:** clear rules if the applicant withdraws before the assessment is performed (`08` §3.1).
- Fee is **not** deducted from any loan proceeds (`08` §5).

## 4. Client obligations

Provide accurate, complete information; authorize data sources; not misrepresent documents. CRP may suspend/withdraw an assessment on discovery of fraud or material misrepresentation (flagged, not adjudicated — legal determinations are not automated, `05` §3.8).

## 5. Liability & protections (`03_...` §5.3)

CRP faces contractual/tort exposure if it uses knowingly inaccurate data, ignores obvious inconsistencies, overstates predictive accuracy, conceals limitations, fails to follow its methodology, lets investors treat the score as a guarantee, or fails to correct proven errors. The agreement therefore includes:

- **Limitation of liability** `[cap TBD]`.
- **Exclusion of consequential loss**, where enforceable.
- **No third-party reliance** without written authorization (a lender's use is governed by its own terms).
- Reference to CRP's **methodology governance**, **version-controlled models**, **per-score evidence log**, **human approval**, **adverse-decision explanation**, and **correction/appeal** process.
- CRP maintains **technology E&O, cyber, and professional-liability** coverage.

## 6. Data protection

Personal data handled per Ley 8968 and the consent/privacy notice (`13`), including international-transfer consent for US-cloud processing. No personal data on any public blockchain (`03_...` §9.1).
