## 1. Principles

- Disclosures must be **clear, truthful, free, and in Spanish** (`03_...` §10.1).
- The **legal lender** is named and identified as the credit provider; capitalYA/CRP are identified as **technology/assessment** providers, not lenders (`05`).
- No prohibited terms ("approved/pre-approved/guaranteed/investment-grade/low-risk") in any borrower-facing material (`05` §3.5).
- Consumer-credit rules are clearest where the borrower is a **consumer**; SME/commercial borrowers may have fewer statutory protections, but misleading-advertising, data-protection, contract, and unfair-practice rules still apply — the program discloses fully regardless (`03_...` §10.1).

## 2. Loan/credit disclosure — required content

Presented by/for the **Lender** before the borrower commits (`03_...` §10.1):

| # | Disclosure |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Legal lender** (name, authorization/registration) |
| 2 | Total financed amount |
| 3 | Currency (CRC/USD) |
| 4 | **Nominal and effective** interest rates |
| 5 | Total cost of credit (all-in, including fees) |
| 6 | Fees and commissions (itemized — incl. capitalYA/CRP fees where borrower-paid) |
| 7 | Payment schedule |
| 8 | Late charges |
| 9 | Collateral (if any) |
| 10 | Default consequences |
| 11 | Collection practices |
| 12 | Prepayment / early-repayment rights |
| 13 | Complaint channels (and which party owns which complaint — `06` §9) |
| 14 | Data uses (link to `13` consent/privacy) |
| 15 | **Whether the CRP readiness score affects eligibility** |
| 16 | **How to dispute** data, the score, or the decision (routing per `06` §9) |

**Interest-cap note:** the effective rate + all fees are tested against BCCR caps for the currency and semester; fees may not be used to evade caps (`08` §4). Contracts may not contain abusive clauses (`03_...` §10.1).

## 3. Readiness-score rights disclosure (CRP)

Even absent a statute using the phrase, CRP discloses and implements (`03_...` §10.1 readiness-score rights):

- **Notice** that a readiness score was used.
- **Key adverse factors** behind the score.
- **Access** to the underlying submitted data.
- **Correction** process for data errors.
- **Human review** (a person, not automation, stands behind the outcome).
- **Reassessment** after remediation.
- **Model-version record** (which methodology version scored them).
- **Protection against prohibited discrimination** (no protected-class attributes in scoring; `05` §3).

The **mandatory limitation statement** (`05` §5) appears wherever the score is shown.

## 4. Advertising & communications rules

Advertising must be clear, timely, and non-misleading (`03_...` §10.1). No implication of guaranteed funding, approval, or rates by capitalYA/CRP. Any yield/return language is prohibited in Horizon 1 (no investor products; `05` §3.6).

## 5. Consent linkage

Data-use disclosures cross-reference the **consent and privacy notice** (`13`), including express **international-transfer consent** for US-cloud processing (`03_...` §9.1). Withdrawal of consent and its effects are disclosed.

## 6. Delivery & record

- Disclosures delivered before commitment; borrower acknowledgement captured and timestamped (audit log, CRP `36`).
- Versioned; each borrower record stores the disclosure version shown.
- Spanish operative text drafted by counsel; English is reference only.
