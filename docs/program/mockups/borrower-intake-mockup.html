Get your business ready for capital — and connected to a licensed lender.

capitalYA organizes your documents, assesses how prepared your business is, and presents your complete file to Financiera del Valle S.A., a supervised Costa Rican lender who makes its own independent decision.

What capitalYA does

  • Collects and organizes your documents once
  • Assesses your capital readiness (CRP)
  • Shows you exactly what to fix to improve
  • Presents your file to the lender, with your consent
  • Keeps you informed at every step

What capitalYA is not

  • Not a lender — Financiera del Valle decides and lends
  • Never approves or declines your credit
  • Never sets your interest rate or terms
  • Never touches the loan money — funds move only through the lender's accounts
  • Never guarantees financing

What it costs you — nothing from capitalYA

capitalYA and CRP charge you no fees.
The platform and assessment are paid for by the lender, business-to-business.		$0 to you
Your entire cost is the lender's disclosed interest rate and the lender's own charges — presented to you in full, in Spanish, before you sign anything.
Nothing is ever deducted from your loan by capitalYA, and no platform charge is a condition of your credit.
Production interface is Spanish-first with counsel-approved legal text. All companies and figures in this mockup are fictitious.

Your data, your authorization

Costa Rican law (Ley 8968) requires your express consent before we process or share your information. Each authorization below is separate — and you can withdraw it at any time.

Readiness assessment REQUIRED capitalYA and CRP may process my business information and documents to produce a capital-readiness assessment. Controller identity, purpose, recipients, my access/correction/deletion rights, and the consequences of refusal are described in the privacy notice.
International processing REQUIRED I expressly authorize processing and storage of my data on secure cloud infrastructure in the United States, encrypted in transit and at rest, under the controller–processor terms in the privacy notice. Recipient categories and countries are identified there.
Share my file with Financiera del Valle S.A. REQUIRED FOR LENDING When my assessment is complete, capitalYA may share my application, documents, and readiness report with Financiera del Valle S.A. — scope share_with_lender:FDV — so the lender can perform its own independent underwriting.
Product updates OPTIONAL capitalYA may contact me about relevant services. Declining this has no effect on my application or my assessment.
No data is shared with any lender until you check the sharing authorization. Your consent record is timestamped and scoped; withdrawal revokes active document access. Nothing you provide is ever written to a public blockchain.
