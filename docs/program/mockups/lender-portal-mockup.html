The boundary, made visible

Use the role selector (top right) and switch between Lender — Underwriter and capitalYA — Ops. Every decision, KYC, terms, disbursement, and servicing control disappears for capitalYA roles — they are not rendered at all, not merely disabled. That is the product expression of the program's regulatory position:

  • Only the licensed lender records credit decisions, terms, disbursements, and servicing states.
  • The CRP readiness score always appears behind its limitation statement, with evidence confidence as a separate axis and no recommended amount or rate anywhere.
  • capitalYA's own output is a completeness validation with final_credit_decision: null.
  • No payment can be initiated here; the lender's ledger is the system of record and reconciliation is idempotent.
  • Sanctions matches escalate to the lender's MLRO — the portal offers no “clear match” control to anyone.
