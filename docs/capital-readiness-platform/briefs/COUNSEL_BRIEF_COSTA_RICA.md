## 1. What the platform does — and deliberately does not do

We are building a software platform that assesses **how prepared a Costa Rican business is to seek private financing** (typically USD $500k–$2M). The platform:

- Collects and organizes the business's corporate, financial, tax, and legal documents in a secure workspace;
- Verifies and analyzes that evidence (software-assisted, human-reviewed) and produces a **Capital Readiness Score (0–100)** with a written report and improvement plan;
- With the business's **explicit consent**, shares the organized package with specific, pre-vetted private capital providers (private-credit funds, family offices, banks), who then conduct their **own independent underwriting** entirely outside the platform.

The platform **does not**: lend money; hold, transmit, or settle funds; custody anything; approve credit or investments; guarantee or promise funding; execute loan agreements; issue or tokenize securities; operate any market; or provide legal, tax, accounting, or investment advice. Every score is delivered with an explicit statement that it is **not** a credit rating, default probability, approval, or recommendation.

**Pilot commercial model (decided):** the business pays a single flat, disclosed assessment fee (~USD $2.5k–5k; discounted/free for the first cohort). No success fees, no percentage-of-funding fees, no fees from capital providers during the pilot.

**Entity posture (decided, pending your advice):** one operating company runs the pilot; no Costa Rican subsidiary unless you advise it is required.

**Data posture (decided, pending your advice):** borrower documents and beneficial-owner identity data stored encrypted in a US cloud region, with explicit borrower consent to cross-border transfer; we can relocate storage if required.

**Scope limits for the pilot:** business-purpose financing only; no consumers; no sole proprietors (personas físicas con actividad lucrativa are excluded); Costa Rica only.

## 2. The questions we need answered (deliverable: written memo per item)

| # | Question | Why it matters to us |
|---|---|---|
| CR-L1 | **Regulatory perimeter memo:** given the activities and flat-fee model above, does any platform activity constitute lending, financial intermediation, brokerage, securities placement/solicitation, investment advice, crowdfunding, payments, trust, or credit-bureau activity requiring licensing, registration, or conduct rules? Please also state what we may and may not say in marketing (e.g., sufficiency of our "no pre-approval, no funding promise" language) | Gates the entire pilot; blocks sharing packages with capital providers until answered |
| CR-L2 | Does Law 7786 (as amended) or related SUGEF registration for designated non-financial activities apply to any platform or pilot-adjacent activity? | Registration duty check |
| CR-L3 | Under Ley 8968 / PRODHAB: required consent texts (Spanish), any database-registration duties, and conditions for cross-border transfer of borrower and beneficial-owner personal data to US-region cloud infrastructure. Please review/draft our consent and privacy-notice wording | Blocks handling real borrower data |
| CR-L4 | Registro de Transparencia y Beneficiarios Finales (RTBF): what beneficial-ownership evidence may we lawfully request/receive from businesses, and what is standard practice for verification? | Shapes our ownership-verification checklist |
| CR-L5 | Garantías mobiliarias regime: reliability of lien searches, and perfection/priority mechanics per collateral class (receivables, equipment, inventory) — what search evidence should we treat as authoritative? | Shapes our collateral-evidence standards |
| CR-L6 | Credit information: under what conditions (if any) could the platform, with borrower consent, lawfully access CIC or private bureau data? (Not needed for pilot — planning question) | Future feature gate |
| CR-L7 | E-signature (Ley 8454): validity of foreign e-sign vendors (DocuSign-class) vs. firma digital certificada for (a) borrower attestations, (b) consents, (c) our commercial agreements. Wet-ink fallback acceptable? | Blocks our attestation flow design |
| CR-L8 | Treatment of fideicomisos and trust structures as applicant businesses or guarantee vehicles | Intake configuration |
| CR-L9 | Ley 7472 (consumer protection): does excluding consumers and sole proprietors keep the pilot outside its scope? Any residual duties toward micro/small enterprises? | Confirms our exclusion strategy |

**Also requested (platform-level):** review of our terms of service, capital-provider agreement (confidentiality, independent-underwriting acknowledgment, liability limits), and Spanish wording of our standard limitation statement.
