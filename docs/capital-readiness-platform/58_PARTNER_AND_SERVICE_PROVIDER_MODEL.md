## 1. Partner types & platform value

| Type (role) | Services | Evidence effect |
|---|---|---|
| Legal partner (R-LP) | corporate/collateral/enforceability review, opinions, title studies | outputs = E3 evidence (22 §3); GATE-01/E-dimension support |
| Compliance partner (R-CP) | KYC/KYB support, screening, source-of-funds review | E3/E2 for F controls; disposition support (final call stays R-CR — D-07) |
| Accounting/financial-review partner (R-AP) | statement review/compilation, normalization, QoE-lite | E3 for A/B controls; CAP-01 remediation path |
| Other approved (appraisers, surveyors, insurance) | valuations, inspections | E3 for H controls |

Partners **serve borrowers or the platform per engagement**; the platform coordinates, never provides the professional service itself (02 §2.6).

## 2. Onboarding (mirrors 54)

Credential verification (license/colegio registration per country — CR specifics 60), KYB + sanctions screen, professional-liability insurance evidence, agreement (confidentiality, scope, data protection, no-poach of platform relationship, quality standards, fee disclosure), provisioning (deal-scoped R-LP/R-CP/R-AP access only — 12). Partner register maintained per country overlay (63: approved-partner list is overlay content).

## 3. Engagement workflow

1. Need identified (checklist item requiring E3, remediation action, review escalation).
2. Borrower chooses: own professional (verified credentials) or platform-referred partner (≥2 options offered where available — no steering; referral ≠ endorsement disclaimer).
3. Engagement contracted borrower↔partner directly (platform is not a party to professional advice); scope template provided.
4. Partner granted deal-scoped access to the relevant sections; uploads deliverable (becomes E3 evidence); reviewer consumes it (52).
5. Completion → access auto-expires; deliverable quality feedback recorded (partner scorecard).

## 4. Conflicts & independence

Partner cannot serve both a borrower and a reviewing role on the same workspace; partner conflicts declared per engagement (12 §7); partner fees never contingent on score/band outcomes (D-15); platform referral fees (if adopted, OQ-01) disclosed to the borrower in writing before engagement and reported on the fee-disclosure page (FR-ADM-03).

## 5. Quality management

Scorecard per partner: timeliness, deliverable acceptance rate, reviewer feedback; annual re-verification of credentials/insurance; removal for quality/integrity issues (register updated, in-flight engagements transitioned).
