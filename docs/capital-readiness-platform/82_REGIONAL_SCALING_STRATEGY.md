## 1. Scaling thesis

The core asset — methodology + evidence discipline + governance — is country-agnostic by design (CRF §3.2 comparability); countries are overlays (63). Expansion cost per country ≈ counsel validation + evidence localization + partner network + provider demand — not engineering rebuild. The 77 quality-bar row "expands beyond CR without rebuilding" is the standing architectural test.

## 2. Country selection framework

Score candidates on: provider demand (existing design partners' mandates), SME credit gap size, registry/verification infrastructure quality, legal enforceability of security interests, data-protection regime clarity, political/currency stability (context library implications), language (Spanish-first leverage; Brazil = major localization + Portuguese + different legal family — treat as its own program), partner availability. **Rule:** enter where ≥2 committed capital providers already want opportunities from that market — demand-led, not map-led.

## 3. Expansion waves (indicative)

| Wave | Countries | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (MVP) | Costa Rica | D-01 |
| 2 (Phase 4) | Panama, El Salvador | prompt-defined; regional adjacency; USD economies |
| 3 | Guatemala, Dominican Republic, Uruguay | provider-demand candidates; assess via §2 framework |
| 4 | Colombia, Peru, Chile, Mexico | larger markets, heavier regulatory mapping; possible local-entity requirements (OQ-02) |
| 5 | Brazil | separate program: language, legal system, scale |

Waves 3–5 are hypotheses; each entry requires the 63 §4 playbook and a §2 scorecard memo.

## 4. Operating model at scale

- **Hub-and-spoke ops:** central methodology board + review hub; in-country compliance/partner leads as volume justifies (hire trigger per 50 §6 per country).
- **Reviewer specialization:** jurisdiction certification for reviewers (training on overlay specifics) recorded in Membership metadata; queues route by jurisdiction competence.
- **Partner network as moat:** per-country approved-partner registers (58) become part of the standard's credibility.
- **Provider mandates spanning countries:** ENT-28 already jurisdiction-aware; packages comparable across markets is the core selling point (same score meaning everywhere — overlay never changes methodology, 63 §2).
- **Language:** ES core; EN provider-facing; PT only with Brazil program.

## 5. Scale risks & controls

Perimeter divergence (GATE-07 per country, 64 re-validation triggers); quality dilution across reviewers (inter-rater sampling 52 §4 scaled; golden-case exams per jurisdiction); context-library maintenance burden (quarterly refresh ownership per country); regulatory attention as visibility grows (proactive supervisor engagement strategy per country — counsel-led; platform posture honesty per 02 §3). Multi-region infrastructure (data residency per OQ-04 outcomes) is the main engineering scale item — kept swappable by ADR-006.

## 6. Success criteria per country (12 months post-activation)

≥15 assessments completed; ≥2 active providers; remediation conversion ≥ CR baseline; zero perimeter incidents; unit economics per OQ-01 model; counsel watch items current.
